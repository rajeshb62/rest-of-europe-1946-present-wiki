История внешней политики СССР 1917–1980, Том второй (1945–1980 гг.)

А. А. Громыко, Б. Н. Пономарев (ред.) — История внешней политики СССР 1917–1980. В двух томах. Том второй (1945–1980 гг.) (History of the Foreign Policy of the USSR 1917–1980, in two volumes, Volume Two 1945–1980). Fourth edition, revised and supplemented, Издательство «Наука», Москва, 1981. The Institute of History of the USSR, Academy of Sciences of the USSR. The second volume of the official Soviet history of Soviet foreign policy, covering the postwar years through the close of the 1970s.

EditorsА. А. Громыко (A. A. Gromyko, Minister of Foreign Affairs) and Б. Н. Пономарев (B. N. Ponomarev); second-volume authors А. Л. Адамишин, А. М. Александров, А. И. Блатов, А. В. Горев, В. Ф. Грубяков, И. Н. Земсков, В. Л. Исраэлян, М. С. Капица, И. К. Кобляков, Л. Н. Кутаков, А. А. Рощин, Ш. П. Санакоев, С. Л. Тихвинский, В. М. Фалин

Period1945–1980: from the end of the Second World War and the postwar settlement through the adoption of the 1977 Constitution and the implementation of the foreign-policy programme of the XXV Party Congress (1976–1980)
NoteThe volume continues the numbering of the first volume (1917–1945) from Chapter XIV; it is dedicated to the achievement of a just postwar settlement, the deepening of Soviet relations with the socialist states, mutually advantageous relations with the capitalist states, and aid to peoples fighting for independence

Contents. The second volume of the official Soviet history of foreign policy, continuing the narrative of the first (1917–1945) from the end of the Second World War. It opens with the general international situation and the position of the USSR after the war (XIV), the peace settlement with the former allies of Germany (XV), and the formation of the world socialist system and the new type of international relations (XVI). It then treats the German question in the first postwar years 1945–1949 (XVII), the Soviet struggle for disarmament 1946–1952 (XVIII), the peaceful settlement in the Far East and the occupation of Japan (XIX), the struggle for peace and international cooperation against imperialist policy and the creation of military blocs 1946–1952 (XX), Soviet opposition to imperialist aggression in the Far East and the Korean War (XXI), and the German question after the establishment of the two German states 1949–1955 (XXII). The volume then covers the end of the Korean and Indochinese wars and the easing of tension 1951–1956 (XXIII), disarmament after the creation of the thermonuclear bomb 1953–1958 (XXIV), the Near East crisis and the Hungarian events 1956–1957 (XXV), Soviet aid to the peoples of Asia and Africa in their struggle for independence 1957–1964 (XXVI), the struggle for a German peace settlement and Soviet relations with the GDR 1955–1964 (XXVII), the USSR and the socialist commonwealth 1957–1971 (XXVIII), and new disarmament initiatives 1959–1971 (XXIX). It closes with the struggle against imperialist aggression and Soviet relations with the developing and capitalist countries 1964–1971 (XXX), the implementation of the foreign-policy decisions of the XXIV Party Congress and the Peace Programme in action 1971–1975 (XXXI), and the implementation of the programme for peace and international cooperation adopted by the XXV Party Congress 1976–1980 (XXXII), followed by a conclusion, a chronological table, a list of the establishment and restoration of Soviet diplomatic relations, and a bibliography.

Character of the source. The official Soviet account of the foreign policy of the USSR in the postwar decades, produced under the editorship of the Soviet Foreign Minister (Громыко) and the head of the Central Committee’s international department (Пономарев) and issued by the Institute of History of the USSR of the Academy of Sciences. It presents Soviet foreign policy in the standard terms of the Soviet historiography of the Brezhnev era — as a consistent struggle for peace, disarmament, national liberation and détente against the “imperialist” politics of the United States and its allies, the North Atlantic bloc and the “aggressive circles” of the Western powers. It is a companion in this folder to the История КПСС (1982) and История СССР, Том 11 (1980), with whose periodisation and viewpoint it is consistent.

Глава XIV (Chapter XIV) — Общая международная обстановка и международное положение СССР после окончания второй мировой войны

The general international situation and the international position of the USSR after the end of the Second World War: the strengthening of Soviet power and international position, the detachment of a number of European and Asian states from the capitalist system and the establishment of the people’s-democratic order in them, the rise of the national-liberation movement in Asia and North Africa, the beginning of the disintegration of the colonial system, and the weakening of the positions of imperialism.

Глава XV (Chapter XV) — Вопросы мирного урегулирования с бывшими союзниками Германии

Questions of the peaceful settlement with the former allies of Germany: the Soviet struggle for the principles of a democratic peace, the Soviet defence of the sovereignty of the vanquished states, the political and territorial provisions of the peace treaties, the economic articles and Soviet defence of the economic independence of Italy, Romania, Bulgaria, Hungary and Finland, and the question of reparations.

Глава XVI (Chapter XVI) — Образование мировой социалистической системы и возникновение международных отношений нового типа

The formation of the world socialist system and the emergence of a new type of international relations: the victory of people’s democracy in the countries of Central and South-Eastern Europe, the development of cooperation and mutual assistance between the USSR and the people’s-democratic states, economic cooperation and the creation of the Council of Economic Mutual Assistance (СЭВ / Comecon).

Глава XVII (Chapter XVII) — Внешняя политика СССР и германский вопрос в первые послевоенные годы (1945—1949 гг.)

Soviet foreign policy and the German question in the first postwar years (1945–1949): the democratic transformations in East Germany, the forgetfulness of the lessons of history in the western zones of occupation, and the collision of the two lines on the question of the unity of Germany.

Глава XVIII (Chapter XVIII) — Борьба СССР за разоружение (1946—1952 гг.)

The Soviet struggle for disarmament (1946–1952): the USSR and the question of prohibiting the atomic weapon, and the struggle for the reduction of conventional armaments and armed forces.

Глава XIX (Chapter XIX) — СССР и вопросы мирного урегулирования на Дальнем Востоке

The USSR and the questions of a peaceful settlement in the Far East: the defeat of Japan and the problems of peaceful settlement, the establishment of American control over Japan, the decisions of the Moscow conference of the foreign ministers on Japan, the struggle for the democratisation and demilitarisation of Japan, the problem of a peace treaty with Japan, the USSR and the San Francisco Conference.

Глава XX (Chapter XX) — Борьба СССР за мир и международное сотрудничество против империалистической политики подавления суверенитета государств и создания военных блоков (1946—1952 гг.)

The struggle of the USSR for peace and international cooperation against the imperialist policy of suppressing the sovereignty of states and creating military blocs (1946–1952): the two lines in international politics, the USSR and the Truman Doctrine, the exposure of the imperialist essence of the Marshall Plan, and the USSR against the imperialist policy of military blocs.

Глава XXI (Chapter XXI) — Советский Союз в борьбе против империалистической агрессии на Дальнем Востоке

The Soviet Union in the struggle against imperialist aggression in the Far East: the USSR against the American policy of dividing Korea and Soviet aid to the Korean people, the preparation and unleashing of the war in Korea, and the struggle for the ending of American intervention in Korea.

Глава XXII (Chapter XXII) — Германский вопрос после образования двух германских государств (1949—1955 гг.)

The German question after the formation of the two German states (1949–1955): two German states and two opposed policies, the Soviet struggle against the plans to arm the FRG and for the fulfilment of the Western powers’ obligations under the alliance agreements, the Soviet plan for a peaceful settlement with Germany, the development of friendly equal relations between the USSR and the GDR, the Berlin conference of the four foreign ministers, the struggle against the inclusion of the FRG in the North Atlantic bloc and the conclusion of the Warsaw Treaty, the German question at the Geneva conferences (1955), the establishment of diplomatic relations between the USSR and the FRG, and the treaty on relations between the USSR and the GDR.

Глава XXIII (Chapter XXIII) — Борьба Советского правительства за прекращение войны в Корее и Вьетнаме, за общее ослабление международной напряженности (1951—1956 гг.)

The struggle of the Soviet government for the ending of the war in Korea and Vietnam and for the general easing of international tension (1951–1956): the end of the Korean War, the Berlin conference of the four foreign ministers, the Geneva conference of 1954 on Korea and Indochina, the USSR and the conclusion of the State Treaty with Austria, the development of good-neighbourly relations with Finland, the Geneva summit, the Geneva conference of the four foreign ministers (1955), the establishment of diplomatic relations with Japan, the further strengthening of fraternal friendship between the USSR and the socialist states, and Soviet support for the peoples’ struggle to win and consolidate their independence.

Глава XXIV (Chapter XXIV) — Советский Союз и проблема разоружения после создания термоядерной бомбы (1953-1958 гг.)

The Soviet Union and the problem of disarmament after the creation of the thermonuclear bomb (1953–1958).

Глава XXV (Chapter XXV) — СССР в борьбе против агрессии на Ближнем Востоке и происков империалистической реакции в Венгрии (1956—1957 гг.)

The USSR in the struggle against aggression in the Near East and the intrigues of imperialist reaction in Hungary (1956–1957): Soviet support for the Arab peoples and the ending of the aggression of England, France and Israel against Egypt, the counter-revolutionary revolt in Hungary, and Soviet opposition to imperialist policy in the Near and Middle East after the Suez crisis.

Глава XXVI (Chapter XXVI) — Помощь Советского Союза народам Азии и Африки в борьбе за независимость (1957—1964 гг.)

Soviet aid to the peoples of Asia and Africa in their struggle for independence (1957–1964): Soviet assistance in the liquidation of colonialism, aid in repelling imperialist aggression against the Arab countries, support for Indonesia, the Geneva conference on Laos, the events in the Congo and the Soviet position, the USSR and the independence of Cyprus, and the development of political ties and Soviet economic aid to the young states of Asia and Africa.

Глава XXVII (Chapter XXVII) — Борьба СССР за германское мирное урегулирование. Развитие дружбы и сотрудничества между СССР и ГДР (1955—1964 гг.)

The struggle of the USSR for a German peace settlement and the development of friendship and cooperation between the USSR and the GDR (1955–1964): the negotiations on a German peace settlement, the protective measures adopted by the GDR government on the borders with West Berlin and the Soviet-American exchange of views, and the further strengthening of fraternal relations and the conclusion of the treaty of friendship, mutual assistance and cooperation between the USSR and the GDR.

Глава XXVIII (Chapter XXVIII) — СССР и страны социалистического содружества (1957—1971 гг.)

The USSR and the countries of the socialist commonwealth (1957–1971): the further development of economic cooperation with the socialist states, Soviet activity in strengthening the solidarity of the socialist countries, the Soviet Union and revolutionary Cuba, and the struggle of the USSR and the other Warsaw Treaty countries for a solution to the problem of European security.

Глава XXIX (Chapter XXIX) — Новые инициативы СССР в борьбе за разоружение (1959—1971 гг.)

New Soviet initiatives in the struggle for disarmament (1959–1971): the proposal for general and complete disarmament at the XIV session of the UN General Assembly, the Committee of Ten on disarmament and the disruption of its work by the Western powers, the Soviet disarmament proposals of 2 June 1960, disarmament at the XV and XVI sessions of the General Assembly, the struggle for general and complete disarmament in the Eighteen-Nation Committee and at the XVII session, the struggle for the ending of atomic and hydrogen weapon tests (1959–1962), the conclusion of the Moscow treaty prohibiting nuclear weapon tests, the struggle against the nuclear arming of the FRG, the conclusion of the non-proliferation treaty, and Soviet efforts for partial disarmament measures.

Глава XXX (Chapter XXX) — Борьба СССР против империалистической агрессии. Отношения СССР с развивающимися странами и капиталистическими государствами (1964—1971 гг.)

The struggle of the USSR against imperialist aggression and its relations with the developing countries and the capitalist states (1964–1971): the American aggression in Indochina and Soviet support for Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia, support for the Arab countries in repelling Israeli aggression and the struggle to liquidate the centre of war in the Near East, Soviet relations with the countries of Asia, Africa and Latin America, relations with the capitalist states, and the questions of European security in relations with the capitalist countries.

Глава XXXI (Chapter XXXI) — Борьба Советского Союза за осуществление внешнеполитических решений XXIV съезда КПСС. Программа мира в действии (1971—1975 гг.)

The struggle of the Soviet Union for the implementation of the foreign-policy decisions of the XXIV Congress of the CPSU and the Peace Programme in action (1971–1975): the Soviet Peace Programme, the further strengthening of the unity of the fraternal socialist states, the USSR and the capitalist states and the new successes of the policy of peaceful coexistence, the Conference on Security and Cooperation in Europe, the struggle for disarmament, the Soviet Union and the developing countries, and the USSR and questions of security in Asia.

Глава XXXII (Chapter XXXII) — Осуществление программы дальнейшей борьбы за мир и международное сотрудничество, за свободу и независимость народов, принятой XXV съездом КПСС (1976—1980 гг.)

The implementation of the programme for the further struggle for peace and international cooperation, for the freedom and independence of peoples, adopted by the XXV Congress of the CPSU (1976–1980): the consolidation in the Constitution of the USSR of the basic principles of Leninist foreign policy, the further strengthening of the positions of world socialism, Soviet cooperation with the socialist states, cooperation within the Comecon system, the struggle for peace, disarmament, international security and détente, Soviet-Chinese relations, relations with the capitalist states, the strengthening of cooperation with the countries freed from colonial and semi-colonial dependence, relations with the countries of Africa, the struggle for a just settlement in the Near East, relations with the Arab countries and the situation in the Middle East, relations with the countries of South and South-East Asia, relations with the countries of Latin America, and the Soviet Union and the non-aligned movement.

Заключение (Conclusion)

A general conclusion on the character and results of Soviet foreign policy over the period covered by the volume, followed by a Хронологическая таблица (chronological table), a list of the establishment and restoration of Soviet diplomatic relations (Установление и восстановление дипломатических отношений СССР), and a Библиография (bibliography).
