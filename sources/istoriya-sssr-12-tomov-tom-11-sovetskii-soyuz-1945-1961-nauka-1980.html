Structure and contents

Chapter headings below are the reconstructed clean titles (English translation + original). Section sub-headings are given from the volume’s table of contents.

Введение (Introduction)

Introduction. The changed international position of the Soviet Union after the Second World War: the victory in the Great Patriotic War, the USSR as a founding member of the United Nations, the formation of the world socialist system and the deepening of the general crisis of capitalism.

Часть первая — СССР в период восстановления и дальнейшего развития народного хозяйства. 1945–1950 гг.

Part One — The USSR in the period of restoration and further development of the national economy, 1945–1950.

Глава 1. Международное положение и внешняя политика СССР

The international position and foreign policy of the USSR. Sections: the formation of the world socialist system and the change in the correlation of forces between socialism and capitalism; the unfolding of the “cold war” against the Soviet Union and the people’s-democratic states; the Soviet Union’s struggle for peace and the security of peoples — the preparation and conclusion of democratic peace treaties with the former allies of Germany, the Soviet Union and the German question, the USSR in the United Nations; the USSR and the countries of people’s democracy — the establishment of a new type of international relations, Soviet aid to the people’s-democratic countries; the Soviet people at the head of the world movement of partisans of peace.

Глава 2. Переход Советской страны к мирному строительству

The transition of the Soviet country to peaceful construction. Sections: the Soviet Union after the Great Patriotic War — the main tasks of peaceful construction, territory, population, the state of the economy; the reconstruction of the life of the country on a peaceful footing — demobilisation of the army, repatriation of Soviet citizens, reconstruction of state and party organisations, the transition from a war to a peace economy, measures to improve the life and way of life of the people; the elaboration of the fourth five-year plan, elections to the Supreme Soviet of the USSR, the first session of the Supreme Soviet of the second convocation, the beginning of the all-people’s movement for the fulfilment of the five-year plan.

Глава 3. Политическая система социалистического общества во второй половине 40-х годов

The political system of socialist society in the second half of the 1940s. Sections: the enhancement of the leading role of the Communist Party — the growth of party ranks, the activity of party organisations, the strengthening of the ideological work of the party; the Soviets in the years of the fourth five-year plan — the activity of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR and the Supreme Soviets of the union republics, the local Soviets in the second half of the 1940s, the improvement of the organs of state administration, the postwar reorganisation and further strengthening of the Soviet Armed Forces; the trade unions and the Komsomol.

Глава 4. Восстановление и дальнейшее развитие промышленности и транспорта в годы четвертой пятилетки

The restoration and further development of industry and transport in the years of the fourth five-year plan. Sections: ensuring the conditions for the fulfilment of the plan in industry; the revival of industry in the territory that had been under occupation — the restoration of the damaged industrial centres of the Russian Federation, the revival of the industry of Ukraine and Belorussia, the results of the restoration; the further development of industry and transport — the strengthening of the fuel-energy and metallurgical base, the rise of machine-building, the growth of the production of consumer goods, the development of transport and communications; industrial cadres and the labour enthusiasm of workers in industry and transport; the results of the fourth five-year plan in industry and transport.

Глава 5. Борьба за восстановление и дальнейшее развитие сельского хозяйства

The struggle for the restoration and further development of agriculture. Sections: measures for the further strengthening of the kolkhoz order and the raising of agriculture — the tasks of the fourth five-year plan in agriculture, the February (1947) Plenum of the CC of the VKP(b); the growth of the productive forces of agriculture and the enlargement of the kolkhozes — the labour resources of the village, the strengthening of the material-technical base of agriculture, the training of qualified cadres, the enlargement of the kolkhozes; the achievement of the prewar level of agricultural production — the labour upsurge in the village, the development of agricultural production in the fourth five-year plan.

Глава 6. Повышение жизненного уровня советского народа в годы четвертой пятилетки

The raising of the standard of living of the Soviet people in the years of the fourth five-year plan. Sections: the material position and way of life of the population — the influence of the war on the level of life, the abolition of the rationing system, the growth of popular consumption, the currency reform, the increase of the real incomes of the working people, the restoration and improvement of towns and villages; concern for the health of Soviet people — the liquidation of the consequences of the war in public health, sanitary and medical service of the population, the development of physical culture and sport.

Глава 7. Культурное строительство в четвертой пятилетке

Cultural construction in the fourth five-year plan. Sections: cultural-mass work, public education, the training of specialists — the main tasks and features of cultural construction after the war, the restoration and extension of the network of cultural institutions and the means of mass propaganda, public education, the training of specialists; Soviet science — the main tasks of science in conditions of peaceful construction, the strengthening of the link of science with production, research in the natural sciences, the social sciences; literature and art — the main features of postwar development, literary life, Soviet art.

Глава 8. Построение социализма в молодых советских республиках и областях

The building of socialism in the young Soviet republics and regions. Sections: the development of the political system — party and state construction, the liquidation of bourgeois-nationalist bands; socialist industrialisation — the industrial development of the republics of the Baltic, the industrialisation of the Moldavian SSR, the western regions of the Ukrainian SSR and the Belorussian SSR, and the new regions of the RSFSR; the socialist transformation of agriculture — the agrarian reform in the Baltic republics, the preparation of mass collectivisation, the unfolding of mass collectivisation and the victory of the kolkhoz order in the Baltic republics, the victory of the kolkhoz order in the western regions of the Ukraine and Belorussia and in Right-Bank Moldavia; cultural construction — public education, the development of science, literature and art, the rise of cultural-educational work.

Часть вторая — Развитие социалистического общества в 50-х годах

Part Two — The development of socialist society in the 1950s.

Глава 9. Внешняя политика СССР в 50-х годах

The foreign policy of the USSR in the 1950s. Sections: the strengthening of the world socialist system — political cooperation of the socialist countries, resistance to imperialist aggression against the Korean People’s Democratic Republic and the Democratic Republic of Vietnam, the expansion of economic cooperation of the socialist countries; Soviet support for the national-liberation struggle of the peoples — the USSR and the Arab countries, assistance to the development of the young states of Asia and Africa; the struggle of the USSR for the easing of international tension and for peaceful coexistence with the capitalist countries — the growth of international tension at the beginning of the 1950s, Soviet proposals on the German question, Soviet efforts to create collective security in Europe, new Soviet proposals on disarmament, measures for the normalisation and improvement of relations with the capitalist countries, support of the peaceful position of the USSR by the popular masses.

Глава 10. Совершенствование политической системы социалистического общества в 50-х годах

The improvement of the political system of socialist society in the 1950s. Sections: the XIX Congress of the CPSU; the strengthening and development of Leninist norms of party and state life, the XX Congress of the CPSU — the July (1953) Plenum of the CC of the CPSU, the XX Congress, the XX Congress on the international situation and the prospects of world development, on the tasks of internal development of the country, on intra-party tasks, the growth of the ranks of the CPSU, the further strengthening of party organisations and the extension of their activity; the further development of the Soviet state — the composition of representative organs, the extension of the legislative activity of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR and the Supreme Soviets of the union republics, the extension of the rights of the union republics, the improvement of the activity of the local Soviets, the improvement of the state apparatus, the strengthening of socialist legality, the further strengthening of the defence capacity of the country; the raising of the role of the trade unions and the Komsomol in the development of socialist society.

Глава 11. Индустриальное развитие СССР в 50-х годах

The industrial development of the USSR in the 1950s. Sections: the policy of the Communist Party and the Soviet state in industry — the features and tasks of the new stage, the course toward the acceleration of technical progress, the change of the organisational forms of the administration of industry, the improvement of the system of norming and payment of labour; the change in the composition of industrial cadres, the new labour upsurge of industrial workers — the quantitative and qualitative growth of industrial cadres, the new upsurge of socialist emulation and technical creativity of the masses, the birth of the movement for a communist attitude to labour; the successes in the development of industry and transport — the unfolding of capital construction, the successes in the development of heavy industry, the expansion of the production of consumer goods, transport and communications on the rise.

Глава 12. Подъем сельского хозяйства в 50-х годах

The upsurge of agriculture in the 1950s. Sections: measures of the Communist Party and the Soviet government for the further development of agriculture — the programme of the upsurge of agriculture, the improvement of economic relations between the state and the kolkhozes, the strengthening of the material-technical base of agriculture, the improvement of the guidance of agriculture, the development of the virgin and fallow lands; the upsurge of agricultural production — the growth of the productive activity of the agricultural workers, the results of the struggle for the upsurge of agriculture, the reorganisation of the MTS (machine-tractor stations), the raising of the level of socialisation of agricultural production.

Глава 13. Рост материального благосостояния населения СССР

The growth of the material well-being of the population of the USSR. Sections: the increase of the real incomes of the working people and the expansion of popular consumption — the increase of the incomes of the working people, the reduction of taxation, the cessation of the annual issue of mass state loans, the growth of payments and benefits from the social consumption funds, the improvement of the pension security of the working people, the regulation of prices for consumer goods, the expansion of popular consumption; the improvement of the housing and cultural-domestic conditions of the working people — the growth of housing construction, the improvement of populated places, the improvement of trade and cultural-domestic service of the population, changes in everyday life; the protection of the health of the working people — medical service of the population, the successes of Soviet medicine, the achievements of Soviet sport.

Глава 14. Развитие науки и культуры в 50-х годах

The development of science and culture in the 1950s. Sections: the development of Soviet science — the organisation of scientific work, the achievements of Soviet scientific thought; the development of public education and the training of specialists — the development of the general-education school, the training of specialists; political-educational work — the growth of the network of cultural-educational institutions and the extension of their activity, the press, radio and television; literature and art — the creative upsurge among the artistic intelligentsia, the development of literature, Soviet art.

Глава 15. Итоги развития СССР к началу 60-х годов

The results of the development of the USSR by the beginning of the 1960s. Sections: the strengthening and extension of the material-technical base of socialism — the powerful upsurge of industry, the development of agriculture, the successful competition with capitalism, the rise of the standard of living of the people; the improvement of the social-economic and class structure of Soviet society — the development and convergence of the forms of socialist property, the change in the social structure of Soviet society, the leading class, social changes in the Soviet peasantry, changes in the composition of the intelligentsia; the flourishing and convergence of the socialist nations — the acceleration of the rates of development of the national republics, the steady convergence of the socialist nations; the Soviet country at a new frontier — the XXI Congress of the CPSU, the XXII Congress of the CPSU and the Programme for the building of communism in the USSR, the growing-over of the state of the dictatorship of the proletariat into the all-people’s socialist state.

Приложения (Appendices)

Appendices. Библиография (bibliography) — the documentary and historiographic base of the volume, including the works of V. I. Lenin, party and state documents, and the wider scholarly literature; Список иллюстраций и карт (list of illustrations and maps) — the plates and maps reproduced in the volume (photographs of industrial and cultural figures, maps of industrial construction, the development of the virgin lands, the administrative division of the USSR in 1946, and others).
