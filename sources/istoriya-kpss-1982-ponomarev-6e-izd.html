История КПСС — History of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union

WorkThe official party history textbook — a single-volume narrative of the history of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union from the origins of Russian Marxism to the XXVI Party Congress
EditionИздание шестое, дополненное (6th supplemented edition), Политиздат, Москва, 1982; 784 pp.; print run 400,000 (first 150,000)
Editorial leadershipAuthorial collective headed by академик Б. Н. Пономарев; chapter fundamentals drawn from И. М. Волков, А. П. Кучкин, Л. А. Слепов, А. А. Тимофеевский, В. М. Хвостов
StructurePreface (Предисловие), twenty chapters (Главы I–XX) each with dated period band and numbered sections, chapter conclusions (Краткие выводы), and a closing Заключение (Conclusion); table of contents at the end of the volume
Period1883 (Plekhanov’s «Освобождение труда» / Liberation of Labour group) through the XXVI Congress of the CPSU (23 February – 3 March 1981), with planning horizons to 1985/1990 — i.e. 1883 to early 1981
Coverage in this wiki’s spanChapters XVI–XX (1945–1981): postwar reconstruction, the world socialist system, developed socialism, and the Congresses from XIX to XXVI — the Cold-War-era history of the CPSU
OCR noteArchive.org scan OCR; the Roman numerals in chapter headings garble heavily (П/У/УГ/Х!/ЖУ/Хх! etc. for II/III/VI/XI/XIV/XVII), and section-page numbers interleave as noise. Clean chapter titles are reconstructed from the end-of-volume table of contents (оглавление) paired with body openers

Structure and contents

Chapter headings below are the reconstructed clean titles (English translation + original). Section sub-headings are given from the volume’s table of contents.

Глава I. Начало рабочего движения и распространение марксизма в России (1883–1894 годы)

The beginning of the workers’ movement and the spread of Marxism in Russia. Sections: development of capitalism and the position of the masses in the second half of the XIX century; the revolutionary-democratic movement and the first workers’ organisations; Plekhanov and the «Освобождение труда» (Liberation of Labour) group, Marxist circles in Russia, the beginning of V. I. Lenin’s revolutionary activity.

Глава II. Борьба за создание марксистской партии в России. Образование РСДРП. Возникновение большевизма (1894–1904 годы)

The struggle to create a Marxist party in Russia; the formation of the RSDLP; the emergence of Bolshevism. Sections: the beginning of the Leninist stage in the development of Marxism; Lenin’s struggle against populism and «legal Marxism»; the Petersburg «Союз борьбы за освобождение рабочего класса»; the I Congress of the RSDLP; the Leninist plan for building the party and the fight of «Искра»; the II Congress of the RSDLP and the emergence of the party of Bolsheviks; the struggle against the Mensheviks.

Глава III. Партия большевиков в революции 1905–1907 годов

The Bolshevik party in the revolution of 1905–1907. Sections: the revolutionary movement on the eve of 1905, the Russo-Japanese war, the Ninth of January, the start of the first Russian revolution; the Bolshevik assessment of the character, forces and tasks of the revolution; the III Congress; the rise of the revolution, the All-Russian political strike, the formation of the Soviets, the December armed uprising; party building in conditions of revolution; the IV and V Congresses, Duma tactics and the defeat of the revolution; the international significance of the revolution.

Глава IV. Партия большевиков в период реакции (1907–1910 годы)

The Bolshevik party in the period of reaction. Sections: the Stolypin reaction; the Bolshevik struggle for the party against the liquidators, otsovists and Trotskyists; Lenin’s defence and development of the philosophy of Marxism and of the doctrine of the party; the party’s struggle to win the working masses and prepare a new revolution.

Глава V. Партия большевиков в годы нового революционного подъема (1910–1914 годы)

The Bolshevik party in the years of the new revolutionary upsurge. Sections: the collapse of the Stolypin policy and the beginning of mass revolutionary actions; the Prague party conference; the Bolshevik newspaper «Правда»; the Bolshevik faction in the IV State Duma; the party at the head of the revolutionary struggle.

Глава VI. Партия большевиков в годы мировой империалистической войны. Вторая революция в России (1914 год — февраль 1917 года)

The Bolshevik party in the years of the world imperialist war; the second revolution in Russia. Sections: the origins and causes of the First World War, the collapse of the II International; revolutionary work among the masses during the imperialist war; Lenin’s development of the theory of the socialist revolution; the February bourgeois-democratic revolution, the formation of Soviets of workers’ and soldiers’ deputies, dual power.

Глава VII. Партия — вдохновитель и организатор победы Великой Октябрьской социалистической революции (март — октябрь 1917 года)

The party as inspirer and organiser of the victory of the Great October Socialist Revolution. Sections: the domestic and international situation after the overthrow of tsarism, the party’s emergence from underground; Lenin’s April Theses, the VII (April) All-Russian conference, the course toward the transition of the bourgeois-democratic revolution into a socialist one; the struggle for the masses during dual power, the July days; the VI Congress, the course toward armed uprising, the defeat of the Kornilov mutiny; the preparation of the uprising and the victory of the October revolution; the causes and international significance of the victory.

Глава VIII. Борьба партии за развитие социалистической революции и упрочение Советской власти (октябрь 1917 года — 1918 год)

The party’s struggle to develop the socialist revolution and consolidate Soviet power. Sections: the party in the struggle to create the Soviet state, the first socialist transformations; the struggle to exit the war, the Treaty of Brest, the VII Congress of the RCP(b); the Leninist plan for building the foundations of a socialist economy, the first Soviet Constitution.

Глава IX. Партия в период иностранной военной интервенции и гражданской войны (1918–1920 годы)

The party in the period of foreign military intervention and civil war. Sections: the beginning of intervention and civil war, the party’s organisation of resistance; the collapse of German intervention, the intensification of Entente intervention; the formation of the III Communist International, the VIII Congress, the Second Party Programme; the turning point, the defeat of Kolchak and Denikin; the IX Congress, the defeat of Pilsudski’s and Wrangel’s forces, the end of intervention and civil war; the causes of the victory of Soviet power and the lessons of the civil war.

Глава X. Партия в борьбе за восстановление народного хозяйства. Образование Союза Советских Социалистических Республик (1921–1925 годы)

The party’s struggle for the restoration of the national economy; the formation of the USSR. Sections: the international and domestic situation after the civil war, the trade-union discussion; the X Congress and the transition to the New Economic Policy (NEP); the first successes of NEP, the XI Congress, the foreign policy of the Soviet state, the formation of the USSR; Lenin’s last articles and letters, the further development of the theory of revolution, of the plan for building socialism and of the doctrine of the party; the XII and XIII Congresses, the struggle against Trotskyism, the death of Lenin, the Leninist levy into the party; the course toward socialist industrialisation and the XIV Congress.

Глава XI. Борьба партии за социалистическую индустриализацию страны и подготовку сплошной коллективизации сельского хозяйства (1926–1929 годы)

The party’s struggle for socialist industrialisation and the preparation of wholesale collectivisation of agriculture. Sections: the international situation and foreign policy in 1926–1929; socialist industrialisation, the struggle against the Trotskyist-Zinovievite anti-party bloc; mobilising the people for industrialisation; the first successes, the XV Congress and the course toward collectivisation; the preparation of the socialist offensive across the whole front, the struggle against the right deviation, the adoption of the first five-year plan, the beginning of the mass kolkhoz movement.

Глава XII. Партия в период наступления социализма по всему фронту. Создание колхозного строя (1929–1932 годы)

The party in the period of the offensive of socialism across the whole front; the creation of the kolkhoz order. Sections: the economic crisis in the capitalist world and the international position of the USSR; the unfolding of wholesale collectivisation, the transition to the policy of liquidating the kulaks as a class, the XVI Congress; organisational and political work during the offensive; the struggle for the technical rearmament of the economy, the growth of the kolkhoz movement, the results of the first five-year plan.

Глава XIII. Борьба партии за завершение социалистической реконструкции народного хозяйства. Победа социализма в СССР (1933–1937 годы)

The party’s struggle for the completion of the socialist reconstruction of the national economy; the victory of socialism in the USSR. Sections: the beginning of fascist aggression and Soviet foreign policy amid the mounting war danger; the struggle to strengthen the socialist economy, the XVII Congress; the struggle for the early fulfilment of the second five-year plan, the victory of socialism in the USSR, the Soviet Constitution of 1936.

Глава XIV. Партия в борьбе за упрочение социалистического общества, усиление обороны страны (1937 год — июнь 1941 года)

The party in the struggle to consolidate socialist society and strengthen the country’s defence. Sections: the USSR’s struggle for peace and the security of peoples amid fascist aggression in 1937–1938; political work in conditions of socialism, the XVIII Congress; the struggle for collective resistance to fascist aggression in 1939, the beginning of the Second World War; organisational work in industry and the strengthening of industrial and defence capacity; the struggle to consolidate the kolkhoz order; the rise in the material and cultural level of life, the growth of the party’s ranks.

Глава XV. Партия в период Великой Отечественной войны (июнь 1941 года — 1945 год)

The party in the period of the Great Patriotic War. Sections: the treacherous German attack on the Soviet Union and the mobilisation of all forces; the Communist Party as organiser of the radical turning point in the war; the victorious completion of the war; the world-historical significance and sources of the Soviet victory.

Глава XVI. Борьба партии за восстановление и развитие социалистического народного хозяйства. Образование мировой системы социализма (1945–1952 годы)

The party’s struggle for the restoration and development of the socialist national economy; the formation of the world socialist system. Sections: fundamental changes in the international situation after the Second World War; the work of restoration and development, the early fulfilment of the fourth five-year plan; organisational and ideological work, the XIX Congress of the CPSU; the CPSU’s role in strengthening the community of socialist countries, the formation of the world socialist system.

Глава XVII. Партия в борьбе за развитие социалистического общества в СССР и упрочение мировой системы социализма (1952–1961 годы)

The party in the struggle for the development of socialist society in the USSR and the consolidation of the world socialist system. Sections: the struggle for peace against the aggressive policy of imperialism; cooperation with the socialist countries; the accelerated disintegration of the colonial system; the fifth five-year plan and the restoration of Leninist norms of party and state life; the XX Congress; the activity of the party to raise the role of state and public organisations, the XXI Congress, the full and final victory of socialism; the CPSU’s contribution to the cohesion of the international communist movement; the XXII Congress and the Third Party Programme.

Глава XVIII. Возрастание руководящей роли партии в период развитого социализма. Сплочение стран социалистического содружества (1962–1970 годы)

The growth of the party’s leading role in the period of developed socialism; the drawing together of the countries of the socialist community. Sections: the foreign-policy activity of the CPSU and the Soviet state, the consolidation of the socialist community; the struggle for the seven-year plan, the October 1964 Plenum of the CC and the restoration of Leninist norms; economic development 1965–1970; organisational and ideological work, the centenary of Lenin’s birth; the struggle of the CPSU and fraternal parties for the unity of the international communist movement; the XXIV Congress.

Глава XIX. Деятельность КПСС в условиях развитого социалистического общества. Успехи стран социалистического содружества (1971–1980 годы)

The activity of the CPSU in conditions of developed socialist society; the successes of the countries of the socialist community. Sections: the struggle to strengthen the positions of socialism and implement the Programme of Peace; the fiftieth anniversary of the USSR; the XXV Congress; the foreign-policy activity of the CPSU and the Soviet state; the struggle to raise the efficiency of production and perfect the economic mechanism; the further strengthening of the party and the raising of the level of its organisational and ideological-political work; the Constitution of developed socialism and the sixtieth anniversary of October; the Conference of Communist and Workers’ Parties of Europe.

Глава XX. XXVI съезд КПСС — съезд коммунистического созидания и мира

The XXVI Congress of the CPSU — a congress of communist construction and peace. Sections: in an atmosphere of unity, cohesion and efficiency; the international policy of the CPSU; the economic and social policy of the CPSU in the period of developed socialism; the tasks of the party in the socio-political and spiritual development of Soviet society; the party as vanguard of the Soviet people.

Заключение (Conclusion)

The volume closes with a concluding section (Заключение) on p. 758, drawing together the party’s historical path as presented in the twenty chapters.
